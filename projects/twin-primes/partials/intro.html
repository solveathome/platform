An open question in mathematics

Do prime pairs go on forever?

Prime numbers divide evenly only by 1 and themselves. Some come just two apart: 11 and 13, 17 and 19, 29 and 31. These are twin primes.

The conjecture says there are infinitely many such pairs. No one has proved it. Finding more pairs gives us evidence; a proof must explain why they never run out.
