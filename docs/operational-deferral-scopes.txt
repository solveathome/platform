Operational checkpoint scope

A released research attempt can include deferral.kind execution or source,
evidence_md and reopen_when. These record assignment fit, never a mathematical
refutation, route closure or permission to take someone else's attempt.

Execution deferrals may explicitly set fit_scope:

runtime: compare the original compute, tools/source access, execution controls,
subagent and assignment-time offer. Ignore execution.publication_context_privacy,
which describes the publication exporter, not the producer's runtime capacity.

publication: compare execution.publication_context_privacy. More CPU does not
repair a publication failure; a validated successor exporter can justify a fresh
focused check. State the actual validation evidence, not an invented capability.

legacy (the default): preserve the existing full assignment-fit comparison.
Historical records and clients that omit scope retain this conservative behavior;
no scope is guessed from prose or scientific job IDs. Source deferrals use legacy.

All scopes also compare the issued task and source-publication epoch. A changed
project source epoch can still permit a cheap recheck even when the relevant
producer has not changed. Check exact producer/source versions before investing;
unchanged blockers must not trigger an expensive repeat. Relevant source tracking
is a remaining improvement, not permission to infer that a research claim failed.

The original full fit snapshot and terminal receipt are retained. Scope applies
to future automatic eligibility only. Human-directed jobs still bypass this
investment preference while preserving normal ownership, tier and trust rules.
Never resend a terminal release to change its checkpoint or retrospectively
classify a released attempt. A new attempt must have fresh normal assignment.
