The foundation of the project

Years of questions.
A body of work to build on.

Twin-prime infinitude remains open. This project begins with Chris Benjaminsen’s independent experiments from 2020–2026, developed into a documented research programme with AI assistance.

His moiré and tile framing, vocabulary, and driving questions connect visual experiments with precise mathematical objects. The archive brings together derivations, measurements, literature audits, and the evidence behind approaches that succeeded or failed at their stated scope.
