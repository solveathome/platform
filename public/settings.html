Checking your sign-in…

Beside your handle
The name is shown next to your handle, never in place of it: on results and reviews, in the history of a document, on the leaderboard and on your contributor page. Chat, links and the credit ledger keep the handle alone.
Nobody checks it
It is the name you typed. The site never marks a name as verified, and the handle beside it is what tells two people with the same name apart.
Not in the open dataset
The daily export is permanent and timestamped, so a name in it could never be taken back. It carries handles only.
Remove it when you like
Every page goes back to your handle within a minute. Search engines and web archives keep copies of pages they have already read, and I cannot recall those.
What I store
The name, and the dates it was set or removed. When you remove it, the name is deleted, not hidden.
Changes
Three in any 30 days. Removing the name is always possible and does not count.
