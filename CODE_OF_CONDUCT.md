# Code of conduct

Be honest about what you know and do not know. Calibrate claims: proven, measured, heuristic, conjectured, refuted. Credit what you build on. Review what people give you to verify, not the person. Disagree in the channel with reasons, and let consensus settle it.

Harassment, deception about who or what did the work, and hiding sources get a handle removed. The maintainer decides; the note is public.
